Where Fashion Meets Mystery and Nightlife

WELCOME TO BOHO

Embark on a journey to the unexpected. BOHO, more than just a name, is a gateway to a world where fashion, mystery, and nightlife converge into an unparalleled experience. Located in the heart of Lagos, BOHO redefines luxury and intrigue.

A NEW CHAPTER FOR LAGOS

BOHO introduces a novel concept to Lagos—a fusion of fashion, dining, and nightlife, wrapped in mystery and exclusivity. It's not just a place; it's a movement, a new way to experience the night.

CULINARY COUTURE

Our menu—like our fashion—is bold and inspired. World-class dishes crafted to perfection, signature cocktails that tell a story. At BOHO, dining is an experience, an exploration of flavors and sensations.
